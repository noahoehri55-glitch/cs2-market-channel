Valve stopped dropping CS2's old cases, so prices should have climbed. Daily Steam Market data for 26 of them shows the opposite.

Method: daily Steam Community Market median prices in EUR, 15 March to 5 November 2026, collected by the public steam-case-prices project on GitHub. The index is the median of 26 cases, rebased to the first week (100), with a 7-day median to remove outliers. Dreams & Nightmares and Fracture are excluded. The data starts in March 2026, so it doesn't show the period right after the drop change.
